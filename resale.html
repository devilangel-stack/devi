Resale Program

How to Resell Your
Flash USDT

Follow these steps to resell your Flash USDT and receive real USDT in return.

1

Minimum Balance Requirement

You must have a minimum of 20 Flash USDT in your wallet to initiate a resale request.
Ensure your balance meets this requirement before proceeding.

2

Holding Period Requirement

The Flash USDT must be at least 20 days old from the purchase date.
Tokens purchased recently (less than 20 days old) are not eligible for resale.

3

Email Verification

You must use the same email ID that you provided when purchasing Flash USDT.
If you use a different email, your request may be rejected.

4

Proof of Ownership

Provide proof that you currently hold the required Flash USDT in your wallet.
Send screenshots showing:

  • ✓ Your Flash USDT balance
  • ✓ Transaction history (including original purchase date)
5

Sending Verification Email

Send all required proofs (wallet screenshots & transaction details) to:
flasherusdt11@gmail.com
Ensure all information is clear and correct to avoid delays.

6

Verification & Wallet Address Request

Our team will review your request and respond within 30 minutes via email.
Once verified, we will request your wallet address to transfer real USDT in exchange for Flash USDT.
✓ Transaction processed immediately after receiving wallet address.

Send your verification email to:
flasherusdt11@gmail.com

Important Notes
  • ✔ Make sure all details and screenshots are accurate.
  • ✔ If any information is missing, your request may be delayed.
  • ✔ Contact support if you have any issues with verification.
